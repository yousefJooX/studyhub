# StudyHub walkthrough (for everyone): transcript

**00:00**  StudyHub: A calm place for your courses, tasks, timetable and notes.  
**00:06**  Start by creating an account. Your data is private. Nobody else can see it.  
**00:15**  Not from the faculty? Leave the code empty. Everything else works the same for you.  
**00:21**  This is your overview. Your day at a glance: lectures, tasks and upcoming deadlines.  
**00:26**  Add your courses. Open Courses, click add and type the name.  
**00:37**  Add the rest the same way. University courses or anything you study on your own.  
**00:43**  Your weekly timetable. Lectures, sections, labs and your own study sessions.  
**00:48**  Tasks and deadlines. Each task has a course, a day to do it and a deadline.  
**00:53**  You get reminded before the deadline. 1 day, 12 hours and 1 hour before, on your phone and laptop.  
**01:00**  The AI assistant. The first time, it asks for your own free API key.  
**01:05**  Get a free key from Groq. console.groq.com/keys → Create API Key → copy the key starting with gsk_.  
**01:09**  Paste it and click Save and test. The site checks the key and stores it encrypted.  
**01:19**  Write like you text a friend. It adds tasks and timetable slots for you.  
**01:30**  Done, all added. With deadlines, and linked to the right course.  
**01:38**  Focus timer. Open a task and press start. Your study time is saved.  
**01:50**  Bring your Obsidian notes. Upload your notes folder or a ZIP of it.  
**01:56**  The library. Folders open and close like in Obsidian.  
**02:00**  Notes look clean. Math, code, callouts, tables and diagrams all render.  
**02:06**  Weekly review. Hours studied, time per course, finished tasks and your streak.  
**02:15**  Reminders in your own voice. Record a clip for each stage and it plays with the reminder.  
**02:19**  Use Claude with it. Create a connector, then ask Claude about your tasks and notes.  
**02:23**  English or Arabic, on any device. On your phone: open the link and choose Add to Home screen.
