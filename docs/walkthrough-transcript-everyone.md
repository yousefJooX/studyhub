# StudyHub walkthrough (for everyone): transcript

**00:04**  Start by creating an account. Your data is private. Nobody else can see it.  
**00:13**  Not from the faculty?. Leave the code empty. Everything else works the same for you.  
**00:21**  Your overview. Today’s tasks, your timetable, exam countdowns and cards to review.  
**00:30**  Add your courses. University courses or anything you study on your own.  
**00:40**  Your weekly timetable. Lectures, sections, labs and your own study sessions.  
**00:45**  Tasks and deadlines. Each task has a course, a day to do it and a deadline.  
**00:50**  You get reminded before the deadline. 1 day, 12 hours and 1 hour before, on your phone and laptop.  
**00:58**  Your exam timetable. Countdowns, and how heavy each exam is.  
**01:13**  A study plan in one click. Pick your hours per day and rest days.  
**01:22**  Closest and heaviest exams first. The day before each exam is its final review.  
**01:30**  Sessions land in My day. Each one has a focus timer, and your study time is saved.  
**01:38**  Flashcards from your notes. Already writing “question :: answer” in Obsidian? They are picked up.  
**01:48**  Or let the assistant write them. You choose which cards to keep.  
**01:53**  Review a few minutes a day. Again, Hard, Good or Easy decides when each card comes back, like Anki.  
**02:09**  Competitive programming. Link your Codeforces and LeetCode accounts. No password needed.  
**02:20**  Rating, solved problems and your streak. Plus the topics you practise most.  
**02:25**  A problem of the day and upcoming contests. One click puts a contest in your plan and your calendar.  
**02:31**  The AI assistant. The first time, it asks for your own free API key.  
**02:36**  Get a free key from Groq. console.groq.com/keys → Create API Key → copy the key starting with gsk_  
**02:43**  Write like you text a friend. It adds tasks and timetable slots for you.  
**02:52**  Done, all added. With deadlines, and linked to the right course.  
**02:58**  Bring your Obsidian notes. Upload your notes folder or a ZIP of it.  
**03:03**  Notes look clean. Math, code, callouts, tables and diagrams all render.  
**03:10**  Weekly review. Hours studied, time per course, finished tasks and your streak.  
**03:20**  Your plan in Google or iPhone Calendar. A private link: deadlines, timetable, exams and contests, always up to date.  
**03:30**  Study together. Share a course with a code. Friends get their own copy of its notes, tasks, exams and cards.  
**03:40**  Four themes, including dark. Calm, Rose, Lavender and Dark. Arabic or English, on any device.
