# StudyHub walkthrough (for FCSIT students): transcript

**00:04**  Sign up as an FCSIT student. Students of the faculty get the archive, college tasks and announcements.  
**00:12**  Enter the faculty code. It is shared in your class group.  
**00:15**  Pick your level and program. This decides which courses, tasks and announcements you get.  
**00:24**  Your overview. Today’s tasks, your timetable and the latest posts from your class channel.  
**00:26**  College announcements. Straight from the WhatsApp channels of your level, through HNU Board.  
**00:34**  Step 1: add your courses. They come from the college archive of your level, with every lecture.  
**00:41**  Every lecture is one click away. PDFs and sheets uploaded for the course.  
**00:47**  Click Add to my courses. Do it for every course you take this term.  
**00:58**  Step 2: college tasks come by themselves. Every assignment for your level becomes a task, with its deadline and course.  
**01:05**  You get reminded before every deadline. 1 day, 12 hours and 1 hour before. Missed ones show as closed.  
**01:11**  Plan your exams. Add the exam timetable and get a study plan for the days left.  
**01:28**  Step 3: bring your Obsidian notes. Upload your notes folder or a ZIP, then link each course to its folder.  
**01:32**  The course page. Its tasks, college lectures and your notes in one place.  
**01:34**  Share it with your class. One code gives classmates their own copy of the notes, exams and flashcards.  
**01:47**  Flashcards for every course. Spaced repetition before the exam, made from your notes.  
**01:57**  Competitive programming, built in. Codeforces and LeetCode stats, a daily problem and upcoming contests.  
**02:07**  Deadlines in your phone calendar. College tasks, lectures and exams in Google or iPhone Calendar.  
**02:13**  The faculty archive bot on Telegram. One tap from the top bar.
