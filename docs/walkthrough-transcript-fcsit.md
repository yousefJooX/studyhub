# StudyHub walkthrough (for FCSIT students): transcript

**00:00**  StudyHub: For HNU FCSIT students: your level’s courses, tasks and archive.  
**00:06**  Sign up as an FCSIT student. Students of the faculty get the archive, college tasks and announcements.  
**00:15**  Enter the faculty code. It is shared in your class group.  
**00:17**  Pick your level and program. This decides which courses, tasks and announcements you get.  
**00:25**  Your overview, with college announcements. The latest posts from your class channel, for your level and program.  
**00:33**  Your level’s courses and tasks arrive on their own. Synced from HNU Board every few minutes, or right now with Sync now.  
**00:40**  All your level’s courses, already added. Straight from the faculty’s course list for Level 4 · Robotics.  
**00:45**  Every assignment from the class channel. With deadline, course and a link to the post.  
**00:50**  Open ones stay open, old ones show as closed. Closed ones are not counted as your work in the weekly review.  
**00:58**  The college archive. Lectures and files of every course, opened at your level.  
**01:03**  Open any lecture. PDFs and slides from the archive, one click away.  
**01:10**  Link an archive course to yours. Its files then show inside your course page.  
**01:18**  Changed level or program?. Update it here any time.  
**01:24**  Everything else works like the general walkthrough. Timetable, focus timer, notes, weekly review and the AI assistant.
