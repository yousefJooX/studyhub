# StudyHub walkthrough (for FCSIT students): transcript

**00:00**  StudyHub: For HNU FCSIT students: your level’s courses, tasks and archive.  
**00:06**  Sign up as an FCSIT student. Students of the faculty get the archive, college tasks and announcements.  
**00:12**  Enter the faculty code. It is shared in your class group.  
**00:14**  Pick your level and program. This decides which courses, tasks and announcements you get.  
**00:20**  Your overview. Today’s tasks, your timetable and the latest posts from your class channel.  
**00:23**  College announcements. Straight from the WhatsApp channels of your level, through HNU Board.  
**00:28**  Step 1: add your courses. Your courses come from the college archive of your level.  
**00:31**  The archive opens at your level. Level 4 · Robotics: this term’s courses with all their lectures.  
**00:35**  Every lecture is one click away. PDFs and slides uploaded for the course.  
**00:39**  Click Add to my courses. Do it for every course you are taking this term.  
**00:55**  Your courses are ready. Each one keeps its lectures, tasks, notes and timetable together.  
**00:57**  Taking something outside college? Add it with Course outside college, like a German or ML course.  
**01:02**  Step 2: college tasks come by themselves. Every assignment posted for your level becomes a task, synced every few minutes.  
**01:07**  With deadline, course and the post link. You get reminded 1 day, 12 hours and 1 hour before the deadline.  
**01:11**  Missed ones show as closed. They are not counted as your work in the weekly review.  
**01:17**  Step 3: bring your Obsidian notes. Upload your notes folder, or a ZIP of it.  
**01:22**  Link each course to its notes folder. Edit the course and pick the folder.  
**01:29**  The course page. Its tasks, college lectures and your notes in one place.  
**01:31**  College files of the course. From the archive, always the latest.  
**01:36**  Your notes for the course. Everything from its linked Obsidian folder.  
**01:40**  Notes look like in Obsidian. Math, code, callouts, tables and diagrams.  
**01:45**  Step 4: the AI assistant. It needs your own free API key the first time.  
**01:49**  Get a free key from Groq. console.groq.com/keys → Create API Key → copy the key starting with gsk_.  
**01:52**  Paste it and click Save and test. It is checked, then stored encrypted. Only you use it.  
**01:57**  Ask about your lectures. It searches your notes, reads the right one and tells you which note it used.  
**02:07**  Answers from your own notes. Great for revising before a quiz.  
**02:10**  It can also add things for you. Write like you text a friend: quizzes, deadlines, timetable slots.  
**02:18**  Done, all added. With deadlines, and linked to the right course.  
**02:24**  New year or new program? Change your level here and you get the new courses and tasks.  
**02:28**  Weekly review. Hours studied, time per course and finished tasks.  
**02:32**  On your phone too. Open the link and choose Add to Home screen.  
**02:41**  Happy studying! Website: study-joox-friends.loophole.site · Code: github.com/yousefJooX/studyhub
