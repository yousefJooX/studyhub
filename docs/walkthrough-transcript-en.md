# StudyHub walkthrough: transcript (English)

**00:00**  StudyHub: a calm place for your courses, tasks, timetable and notes.  
**00:06**  Start by creating an account. Your data is private. Nobody else can see it  
**00:15**  From the faculty? Enter the faculty code. It is shared in your class group and unlocks the archive and your level’s tasks  
**00:25**  This is your overview. Your day at a glance: lectures, tasks and upcoming deadlines  
**00:31**  Add your courses. Open Courses, click add and type the course name  
**00:43**  Add the rest the same way. College courses, or any course outside college  
**00:49**  Your weekly timetable. Lectures, sections, labs and your own study sessions  
**00:54**  Tasks and deadlines. Each task has a course, a day to do it and a deadline  
**00:59**  You get reminded before the deadline. 1 day, 12 hours and 1 hour before, on your phone and laptop  
**01:07**  College tasks arrive on their own. Every assignment posted on your class channel is added for your level and program  
**01:15**  These came from the class channel. With deadline, course and a link to the post · data from HNU Board by Moustafa Sayed  
**01:21**  The AI assistant. The first time, it asks for a free API key. That takes two minutes  
**01:25**  Get a free key from Groq. Open console.groq.com/keys → Create API Key → copy the key starting with gsk_  
**01:29**  Paste it here and click Save and test. The site checks it works and stores it encrypted. Nobody can see it  
**01:39**  Write like you text a friend. It adds the tasks and slots and links them to the right course  
**01:51**  Done, all added. Two tasks with deadlines, and the lab in your timetable  
**01:59**  Focus timer. Open a task and press start. Your study time is saved on its own  
**02:10**  Bring your Obsidian notes. Upload your notes folder or a ZIP of it in Settings  
**02:17**  The library. Folders open and close like in Obsidian  
**02:21**  Notes look clean. Math, code, callouts, tables and diagrams all render  
**02:27**  Weekly review. Hours studied, time per course, finished tasks and your streak  
**02:36**  Reminders in your own voice. Record a clip for each stage and it plays with the reminder  
**02:40**  Use Claude with it. Create a connector here, then ask Claude about your tasks and notes  
**02:44**  Arabic or English. On your phone: open the link and choose Add to Home screen  
**02:57**  Get a free key for the assistant from Groq: open console.groq.com/keys, sign in with Google, click Create API Key, copy the key that starts with gsk_ and paste it in Settings → AI assistant.  
**03:07**  Happy studying! Website: study-joox-friends.loophole.site · Code: github.com/yousefJooX/studyhub
